## 2. Process — "From application to account"
1. **Apply in minutes** — Short form on any device. No branch visits, no paperwork.
2. **Smart credit matching** — Finds the right lending partner for your profile, fast.
3. **Clear decision** — A clear yes or no with full transparency on terms.
4. **Money in your account** — Disbursed directly, often within 24 hours of approval.

## 3. Why RichPay — "Built differently, for you."
Core philosophy: *the borrower comes first.*
- **Transparent Pricing** — Every fee, rate and charge shown upfront. No surprises at disbursement.
- **Decisions in 10 Minutes** — Automated matching and review.
- **Tenures That Fit Your Life** — 3 to 60 months, depending on product.
- **Zero Prepayment Penalty** — Pay off early with no penalty.
